- **#13 design:**
  - `convex/attemptEnding/` adds the `end` mutation. It requires `confirmed: true`, and the owning Learner, same Pilot Institution, and an Active Attempt.
  - It enforces Scenario decision #5's minimum: three Learner turns, or one Learner turn plus one Clinical Action.
  - Ending is idempotent by Attempt state. Repeating the command returns the same Ended Attempt and its reason, with no second event.
  - Restart and learner ending share one transition, `endAttempt` in `convex/attemptStart/model.ts`. It sets the status, `endedAt`, and `endReason`, abandons open exchanges, and appends one `attempt_ended` event. Restart stays exempt from the minimum.
  - `ownAttempt` adds `endReason` and a backend-computed `canEnd`.
  - UI: an End section explains the minimum; confirmation says ending "is not a pass, a fail, or a grade"; the Ended Attempt states why it stopped.
